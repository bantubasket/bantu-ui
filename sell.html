From Your Shop to Their Doorstep

Start selling in a few simple steps.

Getting your business onto Bantu Basket is designed to be straightforward, whether you’re an established retailer or just starting your journey.

  1. 01

    Apply to Join

    Tell us about your business, where you're located and the products you'd like to sell.

  2. 02

    Get Approved

    Our team reviews your application to ensure your business and products are a good fit for the marketplace.

  3. 03

    Set Up Your Store

    Complete your seller profile, add your store logo and description, and provide the required business information.

  4. 04

    Add Your Products

    Upload product images, descriptions, prices and stock availability. Organise your products so customers can easily find what they're looking for.

  5. 05

    Start Selling

    Once your store is ready and approved to go live, customers can discover your products and place orders.

  6. 06

    Keep Growing

    Manage orders, update your products, introduce promotions and discover new opportunities to grow your customer base.

Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions? Contact our team at support@bantubasket.co.za.

Do I need a physical store to sell?+

Not necessarily. You can apply whether you operate a physical shop or an online grocery business, provided you meet our seller requirements.

Can I sell products from different African countries?+

Yes. Bantu Basket is built around the diversity of African products and cultures. You can apply to sell products associated with different African countries, subject to product approval and applicable regulations.

Can I manage my own products and prices?+

Yes. Sellers can manage their product listings, pricing and availability through the seller platform.

How much does it cost to sell?+

Seller fees and any applicable charges will be communicated during the application or onboarding process.

How do deliveries work?+

Delivery arrangements depend on your store's location, available delivery services and the fulfilment options supported by Bantu Basket.

How do I get started?+

Simply submit your seller application with your business information. Our team will review it and guide you through the next steps.
